CoolWatch

Beverage Cooling Time Predictor

How it works: Enter the five temperature and time values below. CoolWatch uses Newton's Law of Cooling to predict how long your beverage takes to reach the serving temperature you want — and plots the full cooling curve so you can see exactly what's happening.
The prediction assumes a constant room temperature and a constant cooling rate (k). Real-world conditions may vary.
